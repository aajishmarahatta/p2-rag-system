# Calvin Natt

Calvin Natt

Calvin Leon Natt (born January 8, 1957) is an American former professional basketball player. A 6-foot-6-inch (1.98 m) small forward, Natt played at Northeast Louisiana University (now University of Louisiana at Monroe) under coach Lenny Fant. After college, he played 11 NBA seasons (1979–1990), spending time with the New Jersey Nets, Portland Trail Blazers, Denver Nuggets, San Antonio Spurs, and Indiana Pacers. He represented the Nuggets in the 1985 NBA All-Star Game, and retired with 10,291 career points. He is the older brother of former NBA player Kenny Natt. Natt's nickname was "Pit Bull".
Calvin Natt later became an ordained minister. He owns a funeral home in Denver, Colorado.
