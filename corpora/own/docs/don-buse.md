# Don Buse

Don Buse

Donald R. Buse ( BOO-zee; born August 10, 1950) is an American former professional basketball player. A 6-foot-4-inch (1.93 m) point guard from the University of Evansville, he played 13 seasons (1972–1985) in the American Basketball Association (ABA) and National Basketball Association (NBA) as a member of the Indiana Pacers, the Phoenix Suns, the Portland Trail Blazers, and the Kansas City Kings.
