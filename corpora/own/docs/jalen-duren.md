# Jalen Duren

Jalen Duren

Jalen Anthony Duren ( DURR-ən; born November 18, 2003) is an American professional basketball player for the Detroit Pistons of the National Basketball Association (NBA). A consensus five-star recruit and one of the top players in the 2021 class, he played college basketball for the Memphis Tigers.
Duren was selected with the 13th overall pick by the Charlotte Hornets in the 2022 NBA draft. On draft day, his rights were traded to the New York Knicks and subsequently to the Detroit Pistons. He was named an NBA All-Star and made his first All-NBA Team selection in the 2025–26 NBA season.
