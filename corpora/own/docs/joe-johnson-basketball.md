# Joe Johnson (basketball)

Joe Johnson (basketball)

Joe Marcus Johnson (born June 29, 1981) is an American former professional basketball player. Nicknamed "Iso Joe" for his skills on isolation plays, he spent 18 seasons in the NBA with seven different franchises and is one of 52 players in the association's history to score over 20,000 career points.
After two years of college basketball with the Arkansas Razorbacks, Johnson declared for the 2001 NBA draft and was drafted 10th overall by the Boston Celtics. He then spent the remainder of his career at the Phoenix Suns, Atlanta Hawks, Brooklyn Nets, Miami Heat, Utah Jazz and Houston Rockets, making seven NBA All-Star appearances and becoming a United States international. Upon his retirement, Johnson was the last active player in the NBA to have shared the court with Michael Jordan.
